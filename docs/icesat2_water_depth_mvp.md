# ICESat-2 Water Depth MVP

This local-first MVP produces water surface elevation, along-track water depth estimate, shallow bathymetry constraint and stage-storage constraint from a synthetic demo or optional local source. It never claims complete bathymetry, full reservoir survey, or verified river-depth model. ATL13 is preferred for inland water; ATL03 is experimental fallback; ATL24 is coastal/nearshore only.
